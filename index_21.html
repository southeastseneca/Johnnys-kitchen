About

Johnny's Kitchen

Johnny's Kitchen is a restaurant and ice cream shoppe on Route 414 in Lodi, a few minutes off the east shore of Seneca Lake. Owners Johnny and Regan both grew up in Central New York and spent years working in hospitality before opening a place of their own — and the food shows it: simple modern American cooking with a real emphasis on local food producers.

The menu leans into things done properly rather than things done fancy. Smash burgers on house-made brioche, sandwiches on house focaccia, a whole page of Zweigle's hot dogs done seven different ways, tacos, mezze, a cornmeal-breaded cod fish fry, and salads that hold their own. Sundays are brunch only, 8 AM to 2 PM. Vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options are marked right on the menu — gluten-free bread is a $1.50 sub — and there's a kids menu, so it's an easy stop for a mixed group.

Out front, under the giant soft-serve cone on the roof, the ice cream window is the other half of the operation — soft serve, hard ice cream, sundaes, milkshakes, and flurries through the warm months. It's the kind of place where you can have a proper dinner or just pull over for a cone on the way back from a tasting, which on the Wine Trail is exactly what you want.

In Our Words

Our Story

Johnny's Kitchen is the rare spot that works for both halves of a Finger Lakes day. There's a giant soft-serve cone on the roof and a red-and-white building that reads pure roadside ice cream stand — and then the menu turns out to have baba ghanoush, a bun cha smash burger, and an elote hot dog on it.

That's the whole charm. Johnny and Regan built this out of years in hospitality and a long-held plan to run their own place, and the care shows in the small things: bread baked in house, local beef, sauces made from scratch, half portions of every side. Come for dinner, stay for a cone at the window.

— South East Seneca

Plan Your Visit

Tips for Visitors

  • Closed Monday and Tuesday. Hours shift with the season — call ahead or check Instagram, especially off-season.
  • Sunday is brunch only, 8 AM – 2 PM, on a separate menu — don't show up Sunday evening expecting dinner.
  • The fish fry runs Wednesday through Saturday. Fridays are the busiest, so go early.
  • The ice cream window keeps its own hours and runs seasonally — Sundays it's open later than the kitchen.
  • GF, vegetarian, and vegan items are marked on the menu; gluten-free bread is a $1.50 sub — just ask.
  • Sides come in half portions and there's a kids menu, which makes it an easy stop with children.
  • Look for the giant soft-serve cone on the roof — it's the easiest landmark on Route 414 in Lodi. Parking on site.
